# Design Spec: Tropical Vietnamese 3D Island Environments (Night 1–3)

- **Branch:** `feat/upgrade-3d-models` (keeps `main`, tags `phase-1..phase-4`, and Release `v1.0.0` untouched)
- **Parity Guarantee:** Zero edits to `src/sim/`, `src/headless/`, or `game.contract.json`. All central gameplay walkways, spawn plazas, lanterns, jump pads, bridges, and ramps remain flush with `src/sim/maps.ts` (`Y = 0` / Night 3 tiers `0, 3, 6`), while surrounding island clearings, shores, cliffs, terraces, and offshore sea stacks provide authentic multi-layered 3D elevation. Decorative critters/weather disable in `--bench` (`benchMode`) and respect `reduceMotion` accessibility settings.

## Theme Matrix (Vietnam Tropical Night Forests)
| Night | Island Theme | Sculpted Terrain | Flora & Geology | Critters & Weather |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Night 1: Đảo Bến** | Bến Rừng Dừa & Cây Cổ Thụ | Star walkways flush at `Y=0`; raised mossy knolls in wedge clearings (`+0.35..1.6m`), stepped lantern stone daises, sloping coastal coves, rocky cliffs & 4 offshore reef islets + coastal foam rings | Coconut palms with coconuts, spreading banyan canopy trees, wild banana/elephant-ear clumps, bamboo stalks, ferns, mossy boulders + small stones, tall/short grass tufts, yellow/white wildflowers | Subtle tree-canopy fireflies (cool green-gold `#b6f78e`, high `Y`), hovering night moths near flowers, gentle drizzle (`0.35`), low coastal mist wisps |
| **Night 2: Đảo Cau** | Vườn Cau & Đầm Sen Sương Mù | Twin islands with elevated outer terraces & rocky bridge headlands, central stone lotus plazas, reef crags in lagoon channel & floating lotus pads + blossoms | Tall ringed areca palms, rainforest canopy trees, ferns, wild banana, bamboo reeds along lagoon shore, pink/violet sim wildflower patches, mossy stones & grass | Cyan-teal dragonflies/moths skimming lotus lagoon & bridge, elevated forest fireflies, thicker drifting lagoon mist & steady warm tropical drizzle (`0.55`) |
| **Night 3: Đảo Chuông** | Rừng Già Núi Đá & Bão Nhiệt Đới | Mountain foothills on outer rim, faceted mossy crag buttresses hugging Tier 1 (`Y=3`) & Tier 2 (`Y=6`) sanctuary cliffs, stone steps & rocks along ramps, turbulent sea-stack ring | Windswept ancient forest trees, old bamboo thickets, stone-clinging ferns, wind-bent tall reeds & highland wildflowers | Wind-angled heavy tropical rain scaling dynamically with `state.wind.active`, swept jungle leaves across island, distant storm sky flash (disabled when `reduceMotion = true`) |
